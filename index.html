Good to know

What does a Tech Executive VA do?

I do everything a traditional executive assistant does, like managing your calendar, inbox and admin, and I also look after the digital side: your website, email marketing, social media and business tools.

Do I need a long contract?

No. VA packages run month to month, so you can change or pause your plan as your business needs change.

How quickly will you reply?

Starter clients hear back within one business day. Standard and Premium clients get same day replies on business days, and Premium includes priority WhatsApp access.

Can you create a custom package for me?

Yes. Book the free discovery call and I'll put together a package around the work you actually need done.

Do you look after websites after launch?

Yes. Website maintenance starts from R800 per month, and the Premium Website includes one month of support after launch.
